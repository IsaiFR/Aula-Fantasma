# EduPiramyd: animación por capas

Modo: herramienta integrada `imagegen`, edición con fondo transparente.
Fuente final de capas: `web/edupiramyd/source-sheet.png`.
Capas: `rig-body.png`, `rig-hind-far.png`, `rig-hind-near.png`, `rig-front.png` en `web/edupiramyd`.
Animación: `pets/EduPiramyd.aulapet`, `pets/EduPiramyd.gif`, `web/edupiramyd/frames.png`.
Generación: `node scripts/create-edupiramyd.cjs`; el montaje de pasos se realiza en `scripts/render-piramyd-rig.cjs`.

La hoja, empuñadura y pata de agarre reutilizan exactamente los mismos píxeles. Las tres patas de apoyo se animan desde cutouts independientes, manteniendo su extremo superior unido al cuerpo. Las pruebas verifican el atlas final y ambas rutas de dibujo en Electron.

## Prompt de separación de capas

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image is the EDIT TARGET: this exact EduPiramyd cat with long rusty sword. Produce an ANIMATION RIG PARTS TEXTURE ATLAS, NOT an animation sprite sheet. True transparent RGBA background. Exactly FOUR fully separate isolated components with generous transparent gaps: (1) Across the UPPER HALF: ONE large intact static UPPER-BODY ASSEMBLY of the same small blue-and-white cat, rusted Pyramid helmet, blue tail, tattered cream robe, gripping forearm, closed white gripping paw, sword handle, guard and the SAME enormous long rusty blade trailing left shallowly down. Keep these all connected and preserve the hand grip. REMOVE ALL THREE walking/support legs from beneath the robe in this assembly, leaving clean tattered robe edges and no ghost feet. This is a torso/helmet/tail/holding-arm/sword cutout that will be drawn stationary, not a complete standing character. Blade tip fully visible and grounded at its lowest point. Keep reference proportions, imposing long sword and compact cat. (2) LOWER LEFT: ONE isolated complete REAR HIND LEG, from blue upper thigh to white rounded paw, upright with a natural bend, unobstructed, extends downwards from a rounded attachment at top to paw at bottom. (3) LOWER CENTER: ONE isolated complete NEAR HIND LEG, from blue upper thigh to white rounded paw, same size and illustrated texture, upright, slight bend, rounded attachment at top and paw at bottom. (4) LOWER RIGHT: ONE isolated complete FREE FRONT WALKING LEG, from blue shoulder fur at top through a mostly white foreleg to white paw at bottom, modest elbow bend, natural cat limb, upright and roughly same length as hind legs, rounded top attachment. These THREE detached limb cutouts are the supporting/walking legs removed from the torso. They must be SEPARATE from one another and from the upper-body assembly with ample transparent space. All legs point down and paws face right; no clothing attached to legs, no disembodied extra feet, no duplicated components. Same overall pixels-per-anatomical-length between body and legs, so limbs can attach naturally beneath robe. Layout upper half wide weapon+torso, lower half three legs centered evenly side-by-side. Only ONE sword, ONE helmet, ONE tail, ONE gripping arm/paw, THREE isolated walking limbs. Detailed original illustration style, blue-white fur, rusty textures, natural thickness. No labels, text, arrows, outlines, floor, shadows or background. CRITICAL: upper assembly has ZERO ground legs or feet, the THREE legs are separate cutouts below, the ONE gripping paw tightly wraps its handle and remains attached to torso. This rig will make the sword perfectly identical in every animation frame.

Expose three natural blue-fur leg attachment sockets under the torso, with NO legs or paws yet attached. Preserve the one gripping arm and white gripping paw absolutely. Keep the THREE isolated walking limb cutouts below, unchanged. Do not paint ground legs onto upper body.
